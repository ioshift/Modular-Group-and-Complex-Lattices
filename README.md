# Modular Group and Complex Lattices

An expository mathematics poster by **Steven Shi**, prepared for the Year 1 **Individual Research Project (MATH40008)** at **Imperial College London**, 2025/26.

- [Final Poster](Poster.pdf)

## Overview

The poster explores the connection between the action of the modular group $\mathrm{PSL}(2,\mathbb{Z})$ on the upper half-plane $\mathbb{H}$ and the classification of complex lattices up to homothety.

Its central result identifies modular-group orbits with homothety classes of lattices, bringing together the geometry of the upper half-plane and the structure of complex lattices.

## Topics covered

- Generators of the modular group and its standard fundamental domain.
- Lattice bases, homothety and normalization.
- The connection with complex tori and the role of the $j$-invariant.
